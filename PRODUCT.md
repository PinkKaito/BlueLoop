# BlueLoop

## Register

product

## Users

Township water operators and the BlueLoop hackathon team demonstrating stormwater decisions to judges. Operators need to understand pond capacity, available reuse storage, downstream constraints, and the effect of approving a drawdown.

## Product Purpose

Turn the supplied five-condition BlueLoop prototype into an interactive demonstration: sense, predict, approve internal reuse, stage safe canal release, and measure results. Public Malaysian-coordinate weather drives a clearly identified simulation. This is a fully mock hackathon project with no physical integration in scope.

## Challenge and Malaysian Context

Sime Darby Property University Hackathon, Planet theme: water conservation, circular reuse, and climate resilience. Use Elmina Central Park in City of Elmina, Shah Alam, Selangor as the single reference setting. Demonstrate a proposed use case for township landscape teams, residents, and nearby businesses. The real park and its public description provide context; the pond, tank, valves, operating limits, and savings remain demonstration assumptions. Use MYT, RM, mm, and m³.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. Follow the supplied Sime Darby Property themed prototype and its light operational dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid the older dark dashboard's fixed success values and claims of live device connectivity. Do not manufacture radar observations or guarantee mitigation for every storm.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the five recognizable operating conditions from the supplied prototype.
- Show the source, simulated time, and running or paused state together.
- Compute readings and benefits from the same water balance.
- Keep operator approval and safety constraints visible.
- Make the demonstration reproducible without network access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labelled native controls, visible keyboard focus, status text alongside color, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
